Connect your Google Sheet

Reads every row from your Ministry Locations sheet and drops a pin for each one. Re-fetches whenever you hit Refresh, so sheet edits show up here without touching the map.

  1. In the sheet: File → Share → Publish to web. Choose the data tab and Comma-separated values (.csv), click Publish.
  2. Copy the link it gives you (it looks like …/pub?output=csv) and paste it below.
